#OVERVIEW
- This repository contains the code to extract annual GPP, cWood, cLeaf, and cRoot from TRENDYv10 datasets are obtained from the Global Carbon Budget (https://globalcarbonbudgetdata.org/) under scenarios S0, S1, and S2 at 31 flux locations with on-site ring width data. In addition, codes to generate figures in the manuscript "Long-term biomass growth unimpeded by short-term photosynthetic decoupling" are also provided. 

#DATA AVAILABILITY
Eddy-covariance GPP and tree-ring width data are obtained from the compilation provided by Cabon et al. (2022), which compiles eddy-covariance GPP from FLUXNET2015 (https://fluxnet.org/data/fluxnet2015-dataset) and AmeriFlux datasets (https://ameriflux.lbl.gov) with on-site Ring Width data observations at 31 sites. TRENDYv10 datasets are obtained from the Global Carbon Budget (https://globalcarbonbudgetdata.org/). All data used in this study is publicly available. 

#CODE AVAILABILITY
Related codes are publicly available at https://github.com/ngocnguyen99/source_sink_dynamics
